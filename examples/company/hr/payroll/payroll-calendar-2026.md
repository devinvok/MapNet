# Payroll Calendar 2026 (restricted: HR only)

Salaries are paid on the 10th of each month. If the 10th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the last business day before it.

Bonus payments for the 2026 performance cycle are paid with the March 2027 salary.

Overtime submitted by the 25th is included in the next month's payslip; later submissions roll over one month.
