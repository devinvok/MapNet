# Data Retention Rules

Customer order records: kept 7 years for tax purposes.

Robot telemetry: kept 90 days, then aggregated. Raw telemetry is deleted after aggregation.

Support tickets: kept 3 years after closure.

Job applicant data: deleted 12 months after the hiring decision unless the candidate consents to longer storage.

Privacy requests (access or deletion) must be answered within 30 days.
