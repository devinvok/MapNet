# Security Policy

MFA is mandatory for all accounts. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Password managers are provided: use the company vault.

Critical vulnerabilities (CVSS 9.0 or higher) must be patched within 72 hours; high severity within 14 days.

Report suspected phishing to security@brightwave.example, or with the "Report" button in your mail client.
