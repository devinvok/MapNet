# Support SLA and Escalation

| Priority | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (fleet down) | 1 hour, 24/7 | 8 hours |
| P2 (degraded) | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P3 (question) | 1 business day | 10 business days |

Escalate a P1 to the on-call engineer if it is not resolved within 2 hours. Enterprise customers have a named escalation contact in their account file.
