# Parental Leave Policy

Effective January 1, 2026. Owner: People Team (Dana Feldman).

## Paid leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Returning to work

Employees may return on a phased schedule: 60% hours for the first 4 weeks back, at full pay.

## How to request

Submit the request in the HR portal under "Leave > Parental" at least 30 days before the planned start date. Questions go to people@brightwave.example.
