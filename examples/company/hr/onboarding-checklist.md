# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## First day

- Collect laptop and badge from the IT desk on floor 2.
- Set up MFA on your account before 5 pm, otherwise the account is locked.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned by your manager.

## First week

- Complete security awareness training (45 minutes, in the learning portal).
- Book your 1:1 with the People Team to choose pension and health plan options.

## First 30 days

Your manager will hold a 30-day check-in. Probation lasts 90 days.
