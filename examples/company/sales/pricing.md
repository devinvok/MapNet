# Pricing (fleet software subscription)

- Starter: 49 USD per robot per month, up to 20 robots.
- Pro: 89 USD per robot per month, includes route optimization and API access.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, includes a dedicated success manager and 99.95% uptime SLA.

Annual prepayment gives a 15% discount. Sales reps can approve up to 10% additional discount; anything above needs approval from the VP of Sales.
