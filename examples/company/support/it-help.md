# IT Help: common requests

## Locked account or VPN password reset

Go to id.brightwave.example, choose "Forgot password", and confirm with your MFA device. If your MFA device is lost, call the IT desk at extension 4400.

## Laptops

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Engineers can request a 64 GB RAM model with manager approval.

## Software

Request new software through the IT portal. Approval takes up to 2 business days.
