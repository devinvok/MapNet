# Travel and Expense Policy (2026)

This version replaces the 2024 policy.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in most cities and 260 USD in New York, London, Tokyo and Zurich. Book through the travel portal to get the corporate rate.

## Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance (per diem) is 75 USD.

## Claims

Submit expense claims with receipts within 30 days of the trip. Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run.
